# Competition governance

This repository is a research and decision-support aid. It is not a substitute
for the students' own analysis, discussion, judgment, or authorship.

Public Wharton rules checked on 11 October 2026 establish that:

- team members develop and implement the strategy and conduct the research;
- the advisor provides guidance but does not make decisions or complete work;
- teams may not contact the competition client;
- official case, trading requirements, deliverable instructions, deadlines, and
  approved resources are distributed through SurveyMonkey Apply;
- WInS uses approved securities and implements the team's strategy;
- winners are judged on client fit, strategy, research, reasoning, communication,
  and the overall competition experience, not simply portfolio growth; and
- AI-generated material included in competition work must be cited like other
  source material, with attention to its reliability.

Therefore:

1. Team-only materials override public assumptions and this repository.
2. Students must verify every factual claim and numerical recommendation.
3. Any wording, figure, calculation, model output, or code contribution from AI
   that enters a deliverable must be disclosed and cited under the current rules.
4. The team must write its authentic Trading Notes, IPS, and Final Report and be
   able to defend every decision.
5. No automated broker, messaging, client-contact, or submission path belongs in
   this project.
