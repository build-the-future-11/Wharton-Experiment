# RENG-JEPA research contract

Scientific status: **NOT EVALUATED**.

The project tests whether a causal, horizon-conditioned reservoir-JEPA gains
practical low-data forecasting value from stress-conditioned latent worlds and
outcome-matured associative memory. The primary comparison is the full model
against the identical backbone with both worlds and memory disabled. The unit
is the decision date; all eligible assets on a date stay together.

The primary endpoint is paired date-level Student-t NLL. Support requires the
complete valid 95% interval for `NLL(control) - NLL(candidate)` to exceed 0.01
nat per observation without material calibration degradation. Integrity or
provenance failure makes the result invalid; it never becomes evidence for or
against the model.

No protected outcome may tune features, horizons, gates, memories, thresholds,
seeds, bootstrap blocks, portfolio weights, or narrative claims. A valid
negative result is final for that freeze. A genuine implementation defect needs
a recorded explanation and a new freeze.

The research system is local decision support. It cannot trade, contact the
client, submit competition work, invent official rules, or substitute AI text
for student judgment and authorship.
