# Timed pitch script

**Five-minute rehearsal plan, not an official time limit.** Approximately three minutes of spoken material plus two minutes to show evidence and pause. Actual duration must be measured by the student presenters; no rehearsal has been claimed. AI-assisted draft, not a student's personal account.

## 0:00-0:40 - Purpose

The question is not which model produces the most impressive chart. It is which investment decisions can be defended for the client's actual goals. Our working approach separates payment needs from risk-bearing capital, compares transparent choices, and makes uncertainty visible. The client case is not yet in this research workspace, so no final allocation or client outcome is claimed.

## 0:40-1:20 - Evidence

The laboratory's earlier synthetic forecasting win was withdrawn after leakage was discovered. Its features contained information about the return being predicted. The repaired baseline study did not establish a statistically supported advantage over the historical mean. That is a reason to avoid relying on the tested forecasts. It is not proof that a different allocation method is optimal. Complex models and a frozen controller also have unresolved evidence problems.

## 1:20-2:10 - Proposed process

The first step is to extract the client's goals, payment dates, constraints and loss capacity from the official case. The next is to compare a simple invested reference, a fixed cash buffer and a reserve linked to obligations. Cash can reduce exposure near payments, but it also has an opportunity cost. Eligible instruments, portfolio weights and review triggers will need a client-specific rationale before any recommendation.

## 2:10-3:20 - Show the stress tool

This calculator starts with normalized wealth and clearly labelled assumptions. It applies costs to trades, earns the specified returns, and pays each obligation once. It keeps unpaid obligations visible and never supplies hidden rescue capital. Here are an early joint loss, high inflation and an unaffordable spending case. These are deliberately chosen sensitivities, not historical performance or estimated probabilities. Compare payments and remaining purchasing power together.

Presenter action: spend the remaining time in this interval switching between the steady and funding-failure scenarios at 10 bps. Point to the assumption label and first missed payment. Do not hide the failure.

## 3:20-4:20 - Governance

Before implementation, the team must verify the current case, eligible universe and trading rules. Students must make and understand their own decisions. Every executed note needs its original WInS record. The final explanation should connect each holding to a sourced objective and distinguish what the team decided from AI-assisted research. There are no real executions or fabricated teamwork stories in this package.

Presenter action: show the evidence appendix and source checklist during the remaining time.

## 4:20-5:00 - Close

The preparation work gives us a traceable starting point and a way to challenge assumptions. It does not supply a client funding guarantee or an investment edge. The remaining task is to turn verified client facts into a justified, eligible portfolio, preserve the actual decision history, and submit only materials the students can explain and defend.
