# Wharton competition preparation package

**Working copy - blocked for submission. AI-assisted material, 2026-09-27.**

This package applies the Wharton megaprompt to the actual Global High School Investment Competition. The product is a traceable investment decision process and supporting research, not a venture with invented revenue or customers. It supersedes older competition drafts for current preparation. Historical PDFs remain preserved and must not be submitted.

Start with `EXECUTIVE_BRIEF.md`, then `WRITTEN_RESPONSES.md`, `PITCH_CONTENT.md`, `TIMED_PITCH.md`, `DEMO_SCRIPT.md`, `JUDGE_QA.md`, and `EVIDENCE_APPENDIX.md`. `FINANCIAL_STRESS.md` is generated from retained calculator outputs. `DECISION_ROOM.html` is an offline interactive view of those same outputs; it places no trades. `IPS_DRAFT.md` and `output/pdf/WHARTON_IPS_DRAFT_BLOCKED.pdf` provide a source-formatted, blocked IPS draft. `output/pdf/WHARTON_EXECUTIVE_BRIEF.pdf` is the one-page brief; `output/pdf/WHARTON_PREPARATION_PACK.pdf` is the complete internal reading copy (paths relative to repository root).

No client identity, wealth, objective, risk tolerance, eligibility, team member, execution, or claimed student experience is filled in from inference. The current public calendar is in `protocol/OFFICIAL_RULES_NOTE.md`. IPS and Trading Notes instructions were recovered from Downloads and are mapped in `protocol/VERIFIED_DELIVERABLE_REQUIREMENTS.md`. Other Apply materials remain missing.

## Release gates

1. Obtain the current case study, trading rules, final-report instructions and any updates from the registered team's Apply account; preserve each file and hash.
2. Extract client and eligibility facts with source file/page references. The source data should distinguish student roster from client beneficiaries; the old `CLIENT_CONSTRAINTS.yaml` roster section describes beneficiaries, not team eligibility.
3. Verify eligible instruments and real WInS records. The recovered Trading Notes PDF confirms three executed notes, exact original wording and reflections of at most 100 words each.
4. Students review, make and defend their own decisions, replace missing facts, and attribute any AI material used according to official policy. They must not describe AI-generated analysis as their own experience.
5. Check final documents against the actual rubric and rendered limits; obtain explicit submission authority.

These gates do not prevent using the local research and calculator now. They prevent calling a draft final or placing trades without authority.
