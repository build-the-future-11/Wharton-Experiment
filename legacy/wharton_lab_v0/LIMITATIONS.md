# Limitations

1. The full client case, quantitative constraints, current security/trading rules, team registration/roster, WInS records and final-report rubric are missing. Recovered deliverable PDFs do not fill these gaps or authenticate later Apply updates.
2. IPS and narrative files are AI-assisted drafts. Student understanding, decisions, authorship review and required attribution remain necessary. Public guidance and IPS citation exclusions are recorded separately; organizer approval is not claimed.
3. Historical forecasting results are leakage-contaminated except for the labelled repaired exploratory baseline study. Its target mapping is inferred, horizon is one day, and same-host replay is not independent replication.
4. Frozen M11 cash accounting is defective; M12's legacy market evaluation includes in-sample use. No repaired controller or factorial claim is made. Implementations were preserved, not silently changed.
5. The new calculator models deterministic assumptions and two hypothetical sleeves. It omits taxes, specific security expenses, cash yield, settlement, liquidity impact and contributions. It does not model the actual client's facility contribution or a real WInS portfolio.
6. Reserve and fixed-cash rules differ in both timing and exposure. This is sensitivity analysis, not a causal mechanism test or optimized allocation. No confidence intervals or failure probabilities are justified by the hand-selected paths.
7. The ETF cache and recovered instruction PDFs remain local and ignored by git. Their provenance/hashes are recorded; no redistribution rights or exact redownload reproducibility are asserted.
8. PDF generation requires genuine installed Times New Roman fonts; the builder fails instead of substituting a different font. The optional browser QA script uses installed Chrome and Playwright. macOS sandboxing blocked its first launch; an isolated approved run passed.
9. This is a locally reviewed preparation package, not a scientific preprint, certified financial service, deployed product or approved submission.
