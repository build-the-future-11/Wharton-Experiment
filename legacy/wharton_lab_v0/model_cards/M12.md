# M12 Model Card

**ID:** M12 | **LAF-GMJEPA (composed stack)**

- **Inputs:** Market feature matrix; optional liability schedule for planner.
- **Outputs:** `predict_market` forecasts; `plan` allocation weights.
- **Primary metric:** Market MSE; allocation diagnostics in receipts.
- **Ablations:** `no_graph`, `no_memory`, `no_latent`, `no_liability`.
- **Invariant:** Market predictions invariant to liability schedule (see unit tests).
