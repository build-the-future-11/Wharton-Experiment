# FREM S10 failure and repair map

15 October 2026. Repairs below are proposed mechanisms, not demonstrated performance improvements. Measured predecessor findings remain in their original reports. P0 threatens validity or the main interpretation; P1 is a major model risk; P2 is a downstream or conditional extension.
