## Proof and empirical evidence are different obligations

1. The fee equation is monotone in post-fee NAV for the declared costs/weights; a unique root and accounting residual are testable. The zero-trade root must be exactly V.
2. The energy-score quadratic is convex on the simplex tangent space; regularization makes its constrained optimum unique. KKT/reference tests certify constructed instances, not forecast skill.
3. Finite scenario support yields finite moments for finite admitted atoms. Robust optimization is exact only over that support, including zero nominal-mass atoms.
4. Immutable issuance plus strict maturity prevents current targets from entering feedback in the tested transition. It is not a claim that every future integration path has been verified.
5. ES properness concerns the total-return law. Since (g,d) -> g+d−1 is many-to-one, no ES-only result identifies price/dividend decomposition.
6. Profitability, practical predictive improvement, calibration, robustness to unseen shocks and independent reproduction require new valid evidence. None follows from these proofs or test counts.
