audit, strongest baseline selection, exact model equations, practical-margin
justification and Kaggle-native threat model have each survived review. It
cannot pass Checkpoint 3 until exact calendars, cell registry, statistical code,
compute matching and protected boundary are frozen. Heavy execution starts only
after Checkpoint 6 generated and target-platform dry runs pass.
