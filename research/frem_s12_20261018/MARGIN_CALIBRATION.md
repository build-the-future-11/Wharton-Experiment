# FREM-S12 pre-outcome practical-margin memorandum

Date: 18 October 2026. No S12 market archive or numeric market outcome was
accessed for this memorandum.

The five-percent relative energy-score threshold is a prospective practical
filter, not an estimated effect size and not a claim that five percent is a
universal economic boundary. It is retained because S12 adds trainable routing,
24 predeclared configurations and materially more compute than the fixed S11
stacker. A merely detectable or sub-five-percent gain would not justify that
complexity under this study.

The threshold cannot be reduced after development. Generated-data behavior is
used only to verify code paths and failure logic; it did not calibrate the
threshold and cannot establish expected market effect size. Support still
requires the simultaneous corrected uncertainty, fold consistency, family
non-negativity and variogram noninferiority conditions. Policy wealth cannot
rescue a failure.

Disposition: `FIVE_PERCENT_RETAINED_AS_STRICT_PROSPECTIVE_COMPLEXITY_HURDLE`.
