# GYIC current equity watchlist — 11 October 2026

## Verdict

This is a **provisional research watchlist, not a FREM forecast, personalized financial advice, or a competition-compliant allocation**. The repository has no admitted real-market panel or fitted FREM checkpoint. Wharton's public FAQ says the current case, trading requirements, and other team materials are released through SurveyMonkey Apply; the public pages do not establish the eligible-security universe.

For a long-horizon growth sleeve, subject to the team packet and concentration policy, the current ranking is:

1. **GOOGL — highest-conviction accumulate.** Latest observed quote: **$351.66**; trailing P/E snapshot: **17.66**. Alphabet's Q2 2026 revenue rose 24% to $119.8B, Google Cloud revenue rose 82% to $24.8B, and operating margin was 34%. The reported EPS was distorted upward by a $98.0B unrealized equity gain, while trailing-twelve-month capex reached $132.4B. The thesis is strong core growth at the lowest observed multiple in this list; the risks are AI-capex intensity, dilution/financing, regulation, and advertising concentration.
2. **MSFT — high-conviction accumulate.** Latest observed quote: **$535.07**; trailing P/E snapshot: **31.85**. FY2026 revenue rose 18%, Microsoft Cloud revenue rose 27%, and Azure revenue rose 41% for the year. Demand and contracted backlog are strong; the risks are a richer valuation, falling cloud gross margin, and high AI infrastructure spending.
3. **V — quality compounder / accumulate on weakness.** Latest observed quote: **$385.45**; trailing P/E snapshot: **32.80**. Fiscal Q3 2026 net revenue rose 14%, payments volume and processed transactions rose 10%, and cross-border volume excluding intra-Europe rose 12%. The risks are valuation, litigation/regulation, and cyclical cross-border spending.
4. **TSM — smaller satellite position only.** Latest observed ADR quote: **$453.31**. September 2026 consolidated revenue rose 54.6% year over year and January–September revenue rose 41.1%. This is the clearest AI-infrastructure manufacturing exposure in the set, but Taiwan/geopolitical risk and semiconductor cyclicality justify a lower cap than GOOGL or MSFT.
5. **LLY — growth watchlist, wait for price discipline.** Latest observed quote: **$1,179.27**; trailing P/E snapshot: **39.57**. Q2 revenue rose 48%, management raised full-year revenue guidance to $85B–$87B, and the late-stage obesity pipeline advanced. The risks are a high multiple, product/reimbursement concentration, manufacturing execution, and clinical/regulatory outcomes.

**COST is a hold/watch rather than a new buy at the observed snapshot.** The business remains high quality, but the observed **47.63 trailing P/E** at **$946.92** leaves less valuation margin than GOOGL, MSFT, or Visa.

If the team wants a concrete **research-sleeve starting mix** after verifying eligibility, use **GOOGL 25% / MSFT 25% / V 20% / LLY 15% / TSM 10% / cash 5% within that sleeve**. This is not an overall client allocation: the equity-sleeve size, reserve assets, concentration limits, current holdings, and funding-confidence target are still unresolved. The headline GOOGL P/E is also not directly comparable with the others because the quarter included the large unrealized equity gain described above.

## Bounded decision rule

- Do not present these names as approved until each appears in the current WInS/participant universe.
- Do not place all capital into this stock sleeve. Laura's 2033–2042 operating obligations require a separately validated reserve and a diversified long-horizon portfolio.
- If the team elects to use this list, stage entries rather than make one all-in trade, cap single-name and correlated AI exposure prospectively, and write the thesis, falsifier, expected holding period, and exit condition before executing in WInS.
- Reassess the ranking after each company's next earnings release or if the investment thesis changes; do not reinterpret a short-term price loss as proof of a broken long-term thesis without mechanism-level evidence.
