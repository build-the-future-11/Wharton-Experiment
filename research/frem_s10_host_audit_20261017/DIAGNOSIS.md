## Why there is no SOTA claim

The current system has no demonstrated reliable increment over its simple controls and no complete matched-budget external SOTA benchmark. S10 is explicitly a bounded retrospective study. Even a successful S10 result would not automatically establish general SOTA, original FREM NLL success, point-in-time validity or algorithmic novelty.

Context-varying partially pooled mixture weights already appear in Bayesian hierarchical stacking (Yao et al., 2021): https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.08954 . Cost/risk/holdings-aware optimization has established prior art (Boyd et al., 2017): https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/cvx_portfolio.html . Primary pages checked 17 October 2026. Neither paper establishes S10 performance. A separate SOTA claim requires defined task/universe/horizon, strong external baselines with equal information/search budgets, untouched evaluation and independent reproduction.
