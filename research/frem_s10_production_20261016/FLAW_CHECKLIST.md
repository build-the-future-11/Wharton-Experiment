## Remaining evidence that code cannot manufacture

The final candidate still needs its complete observed verification run, actual offline build/install, real pre-2010 precision, generated full-scale qualification and the frozen development campaign. Each depends on the preceding gate. A valid failed precision gate or negative study must remain a terminal scientific observation for that version.

Independent empirical reproduction, genuinely unused point-in-time confirmation, broad-universe validity, actual fills/slippage/payment-date settlement, redistribution permission, algorithmic novelty and final paper/release remain separate obligations. No amount of generated testing proves future market gains. The mechanism still has finite historical support, potentially weak contextual information and the statistical sensitivity limits recorded in the prior register.
